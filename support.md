## Support policy

Dedicated support is available to regular contributors, sponsors, and companies that have subscribed to a custom support contract.

Support requests can be submitted on [GitHub](https://github.com/openiddict/openiddict-core/issues) or, depending on
the selected sponsorship tier, privately by email at [contact@openiddict.com](mailto:contact@openiddict.com).

**Support is offered only for the latest stable version of OpenIddict.** There are, however, two exceptions to this policy:

- **ABP Framework users** receive patches for OpenIddict for as long as ABP Framework itself is supported by Volosoft (typically one year after the release of a major ABP version), whether they have a commercial ABP license or simply use the free packages:

| OpenIddict branch | ABP Framework branch | End of support date |
|-------------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| 6.x               | 9.x                  | November 2026       |
| 7.x (current)     | 10.x                 | November 2027       |

- **OpenIddict sponsors** receive extended support depending on the selected sponsorship tier:
  - Tier 6 sponsors get full support for the previous version 1 month after the release of a new major version.
  - Tier 7 sponsors get full support for the previous version 6 months after the release of a new major version.
  - Tier 8 sponsors get full support for the previous version 12 months after the release of a new major version.
  - Tier 9 sponsors get full support for the previous version 24 months after the release of a new major version.

| OpenIddict branch | Sponsorship tier | End of support date |
|-------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| 5.x               | Tier 6           | January 17, 2025    |
| 5.x               | Tier 7           | June 17, 2025       |
| 5.x               | Tier 8           | December 17, 2025   |
| 5.x               | Tier 9           | December 17, 2026   |
|                   |                  |                     |
| 6.x               | Tier 6           | August 7, 2025      |
| 6.x               | Tier 7           | January 7, 2026     |
| 6.x               | Tier 8           | July 7, 2026        |
| 6.x               | Tier 9           | July 7, 2027        |
|                   |                  |                     |
| 7.x (current)     | Any              | Currently supported |
